I am Atharva Tikle, an engineer specializing in embedded systems, IoT, and hardware design. I hold a diploma in Mechatronics Engineering and am currently pursuing a B.Tech.

My work spans the full product stack: schematic capture and PCB layout in KiCad, firmware for ESP32, ESP8266, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi, computer vision with Python and OpenCV, and web dashboards that connect devices to users. I publish most of my work as open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/sudo-atharva).

## Core Skills

- **Embedded systems:** ESP32, ESP8266, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, sensor integration, motor control
- **Hardware:** PCB design in KiCad, power electronics, battery management, 3D printing
- **Programming:** C, C++, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, PHP
- **Software:** OpenCV, machine learning, React, Node.js, MongoDB, Socket.IO, Linux

- [KiCad Plugin](https://github.com/sudo-atharva/kicad-myplugin) - KiCad 10 plugin providing fab-aware DRC, voltage/current trace sizing, power pours with stitching, H/V autorouting, silkscreen cleanup, and Eagle import helpers.
- [OctoPrint PSU Control - Tapo](https://github.com/sudo-atharva/OctoPrint-PSUControl-Tapo) - OctoPrint plugin for TP-Link Tapo P110 smart plugs: one-click power, auto-off after print, auto-connect, and OctoDash/OctoApp support.
